Queuing regen doesn't act immediately — it arms a rescue for the current cycle. If Shikkari Shambhu's HP drains down to ~100 while regen is queued, he'll be restored to 500 HP and a new cycle begins. If HP reaches 0 with nothing queued, the fight ends. The queue resets at the start of every new cycle, so you'll need to decide again.
